Joshua Evans

Team Leader & Licensed Advisor

Hi, I'm Joshua. I lead a team of licensed insurance agents here in Ottawa, and I'm always looking for the right people to join us. Not just anyone. Licensed agents who know they're capable of more, who are tired of doing this alone, and who want real people in their corner.

I spent 7 years in the Canadian Armed Forces before an injury ended that chapter and I had to start over. I came into this industry with no background and no network, and I learned the hard way what most agents go through. You get licensed, you get a few big promises, and then you're left to figure it all out by yourself. No real training. No structure. Nobody to call when a case gets complicated.

What turned it around for me wasn't talent. It was structure and mentorship, the same things the military drilled into me. So that's exactly what I've built for my team. Real training, real accountability, and a real office in Ottawa you can actually walk into, not another Zoom-only group you never hear from again. Weekly, in person, with people who have already done it.

If you're licensed and you've been grinding it out on your own, watching other agents grow and wondering what they know that you don't, here's the honest answer: nothing. They just have people around them. That's the whole difference, and it's the thing I care most about giving the agents who join me.

Outside of work, I'm pretty easy to get along with. I love coffee, travelling, and any excuse to be outside playing volleyball or golf in the summer. My partner Andrea and I are on a mission to try every Italian restaurant in Ottawa, usually cheered on by our two rescue cats, Luna and Layla.

I'm not here to pitch you. I'm here to see if we're a good fit for each other. If you're serious about building something real with the right people behind you, this is a great place to start. Looking forward to our conversation.
